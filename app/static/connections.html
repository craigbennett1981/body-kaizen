FreeStyle Libre (LibreLinkUp)

Glucose from your sensor every 30 minutes, matched to your meals. It uses a LibreLinkUp follower account that your LibreLink app shares with.

No LibreLinkUp account yet? In the LibreLink app: Menu › Connected Apps › LibreLinkUp › Add connection, then accept the invite in the LibreLinkUp app and log in here with that account.

Garmin Connect

Sleep, HRV, resting heart rate, steps, training readiness and workouts from your watch, four times a day. The password is used once to get a token (kept in ~/.garminconnect, renewed automatically) and is never stored.

Garmin has no official API for personal data, so this uses the community garminconnect library. It can break when Garmin changes its site.
