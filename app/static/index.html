Set up your AI assistant

You log by talking to an AI assistant like Claude: it writes your meals, workouts and readings into your data folder, and this dashboard shows them.

  1. Claude Code on this computer (simplest): open your data folder in it, , and paste the prompt below as your first message.
  2. Claude on your phone, through a Claude project: your data folder needs to be a private GitHub repository the project can reach. How to set that up.
  3. It reads the Body Kaizen guide, writes a CLAUDE.md in your data folder, and asks you a few questions about your training, habits and goals. Then send it your first meal.
