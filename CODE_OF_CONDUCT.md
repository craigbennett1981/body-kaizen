# Code of Conduct

Body Kaizen is about people's bodies and health, which can be personal and sensitive. Everyone taking part in its
issues, pull requests, discussions and other spaces is expected to keep it a welcoming, respectful place.

## Expected

- Be kind and assume good intent. People come with different bodies, goals, budgets and experience.
- Give and take feedback on the work, not the person.
- Respect privacy: yours and other people's. Don't ask anyone to share health data.
- Be careful with health claims. Link evidence, and don't give anyone medical advice.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or demeaning comments, including about anyone's body, weight, diet, health, age, gender,
  sexuality, race, religion, disability or nationality.
- Body-shaming, or promoting extreme dieting, disordered eating or unsafe training.
- Publishing anyone's private information, including health data, without their explicit permission.
- Sexual content, threats, or sustained disruption.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, commits and issues, and may warn, temporarily block or permanently
ban anyone whose behaviour breaks this code.

Report a problem privately to the maintainer through GitHub's private vulnerability reporting (see
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)), choosing "conduct" in the title, or by any private contact listed on the maintainer's
GitHub profile. Reports are kept confidential.

This code applies in all project spaces, and when someone represents the project elsewhere.
